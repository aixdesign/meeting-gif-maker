MEETING GIF MAKER — how to use it
==================================

Turn a call recording into a looping GIF of its best moments.
Everything runs on your own computer — recordings are never uploaded anywhere.


GETTING STARTED (one-time, ~2 minutes)
--------------------------------------
1. Move this folder anywhere you like (e.g. your home folder).

2. Double-click "Start GIF Maker.command".

   ⚠️ THE FIRST TIME ONLY, Mac will likely block it:
   → RIGHT-CLICK the file → choose "Open" → click "Open" again.
   After this once, normal double-click works.

3. Wait a minute or two — it sets itself up automatically
   (needs internet this first time).

4. Your browser opens the tool by itself. Done.

Keep the black Terminal window open while you use the tool.
Close it when you're finished.


MAKING A GIF
------------
1. Drag your recording into the box (MP4, MOV, WEBM, MKV — big files OK.
   Drop several videos at once to combine them into one GIF).

2. Pick your settings:
   • Number of stills — how many moments end up in the GIF
   • Pace — Slow / Medium / Fast
   • Style — Slideshow (held stills) or Motion (mini live clips)

3. Click "Make GIF" and wait — it scans the whole recording and
   auto-picks the sharpest, most varied moments.

4. Want different frames? Click "▸ Edit chosen frames", click frames
   to add/remove them, then "Rebuild GIF".

5. Click "Download GIF". That's it.


IF SOMETHING'S WEIRD
--------------------
• Nothing opens? Make sure you kept the Terminal window open,
  then go to http://127.0.0.1:5001 in your browser.
• Still stuck? Close the Terminal window and double-click
  "Start GIF Maker.command" again.
